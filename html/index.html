A companion app for the Square Golf Omni launch monitor. It connects over Bluetooth, records every shot on your own device, and turns raw launch numbers into a verdict — what's costing you distance, and how many yards it's worth. Built for the sim-room PC first — the one place the official app doesn't run.

§01

Dispersion, with a confidence interval

Every club's pattern plotted as carry against offline, with a statistical ellipse drawn to the convention you choose — Trackman's 2σ (~86% of shots) or the PGA Tour's 90% — so "my 7-iron goes 165" becomes "my 7-iron lands inside this shape."

Overlay PGA Tour launch profiles, built from published TrackMan averages, to see how your window compares to the professional one.

§02

A verdict, not a data dump

The optimizer reads your last shots per club, compares them against launch-condition windows for your speed, and ranks every inefficiency by the yards it's estimated to cost you.

Analysis ends in an action: each session closes with one prioritized, plain-language recommendation — not thirty numbers to interpret on your own.

§03

Flight you can interrogate

The Omni measures the ball once — at impact. Everything after that moment, carry, apex, curvature, descent, roll, is computed by the app's physics-based flight model and rendered as a 3D flight you can orbit, on a hole you lay out yourself.

Measured and simulated are different classes of truth, and the app never blurs which is which — the same honesty this page uses in its badges.

§04

See the swing behind the number

Plug in one or two ordinary UVC webcams. The app keeps a rolling buffer and, when the Omni reports a shot, seals three seconds either side of impact from every angle — down the line and face on, on one shared clock.

The replay opens itself the moment the capture completes, loops at full, half or quarter speed, and exports as a single stitched video aligned to the shot the launch monitor recorded.

Trigger: the Omni's packet
  • PRE_ROLL3.0 s before the shot
  • POST_ROLL3.0 s after
  • ANGLESup to 2, one shared clock
  • REPLAYauto · 1× / 0.5× / 0.25×
  • EXPORTMP4 · stitched or separate
§05

Gapping that shows the gap

Every session feeds My Bag: real carry numbers per club, from your shots, not the loft printed on the sole. The chart makes the flaw visible — a 24-yard hole between two clubs is a decision you can act on.

§06

Your shots never leave your device

LOCAL_DB

No account. No cloud.

Sessions live in a local database on the machine that recorded them. The app works entirely offline — the only radio it needs is Bluetooth to the Omni.

EXPORT_CSV

Take everything with you

Any session exports to CSV — every measured value, every derived value, ready for your own spreadsheet or analysis tools.

PROTO_CAPTURE

Down to the protocol

The app grew out of understanding the Omni's BLE protocol first-hand, and it will show you the raw frames it decodes.

§07

Built for where you actually practice

The sim room — Windows desktop

The official app doesn't run on a PC. This one was built there first, with its own Windows Bluetooth stack, and it uses the width: on an ultrawide monitor the session splits into table, dispersion, optimizer and both camera angles at once.

The mat — iPad & Android tablet

Propped beside the hitting mat and read from address position: big tabular numbers, one glance between swings. Same app, same design, same local data.

§08

Data classes

Every number in the app carries its class. Measured values come from the device; simulated values come from the flight model and are documented, validated and honest about their limits; reference values come from published sources.
